# What prefix training adds

Data seeds [0, 1, 2]. Every curve is evaluated at k = 1,…,32 and cached in `ablation.json`.

condKL (Ours) is prefix-NLPD training with the conditional-KL order refreshed after every step. Naive sparse GP is one full ELBO model: its inducing points are sorted once by conditional KL, and every budget reads a prefix of that same posterior. GVS + prefix uses the same prefix training and the same refresh, with pivoted Cholesky as the order. GVS on Ours keeps the prefix-trained parameters and only replaces the order. KL prune deletes the least informative point from the full model one at a time; for this Gaussian posterior the projection is the marginal, so the kept parameters do not move.

↓ lower is better. **Bold** is the best mean in that row. A negative difference favors the first method.
