↓ means lower is better. ↑ means higher is better. → 0.95 means closer to the nominal 95% coverage is better. **Bold** is the best mean in that column at that budget. Params counts inducing parameters plus the 3 kernel and noise parameters of the EQ, Matern, and sawtooth models. The weak-periodic model stores two additional kernel parameters.
